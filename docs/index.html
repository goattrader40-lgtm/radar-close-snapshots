Graded gappers (5%+, top 8 up and 4 down by pre-market $ volume)

Gapping 5%+ up or down before the open and passing your rules (market cap $700M+, 30-day average volume 700K+ shares, 20-day average $ volume $20M+, ADR 3%+). Scanned at — ET on — (not today: today's list posts by 9:10 AM ET on weekdays).

Grade = how solid the news behind the gap is, from reading the actual filings, releases and earnings: A real fundamental change (earnings beat with raised guidance, big growth inflection, FDA approval, takeover), B decent (meaningful contract, partnership, index add), C weak (analyst call, sympathy move, hype, no clear news), F warning (offering or other dilution, promotional release, reverse split, unconfirmed news). For gap downs the grade means how real and lasting the bad news is: A serious damage (guidance cut, big miss, lost customer), B meaningful but contained, C weak or no real news (possible overreaction). Inflection = a change in the business trend: Turnaround (a decline reverses), Acceleration (growth speeds up), New business (a new product or customer that changes the company), Margin (profits jump on cost or mix), Deterioration (the trend breaks down); scored 1–10 for how big and how confirmed it is. The top 8 gap ups and top 4 gap downs by pre-market $ volume are graded, and only A and B grades show unless you pick Show all. If fewer stocks pass your rules, only those are researched; if none pass, nothing is. EP = gap of 10%+ with pre-market volume already 25%+ of a normal day (Qullamaggie's episodic pivot asks for a 10%+ gap on huge early volume). Gap ÷ ADR shows how big the gap is for that stock. Pre-market prices come from Yahoo's 5-minute bars at scan time; check live prints in TradingView.

Track record of the grades

Every graded gapper is logged, then followed: Held = for gap ups, closed the gap day at or above the pre-market price (for gap downs, at or below). Gap day = close vs the previous close. 5D and 20D = move from the gap-day close 5 and 20 sessions later. Grades only count as useful if A's clearly beat C's here over a few weeks.

During market hours (9:30 AM–4:10 PM ET) prices update about every 5 minutes from Yahoo quotes, and this page checks for new data every 2 minutes on its own. Live 1D is today's change so far; live 1W, 1M and 3M compare today's price with the close 5, 21 and 63 sessions back; Rel vol is relative volume at time: volume from 9:30 to now ÷ the average of the last 20 sessions' volume from 9:30 to the same minute (from 5-minute bars on the consolidated tape via Alpaca, all US exchanges and off-exchange trades), so 2.00× at 11:50 means twice the usual volume by 11:50; it's rough in the first 5 minutes. After the close it's the full day ÷ the previous 20 sessions' average. Everything else (themes and their scores, ADR%, $ volume, 200-day SMA, averages) is rebuilt after each close, around 4:30 PM ET. Newest day comes from: —; earlier days, volume and company details come from Nasdaq's daily list, which runs one session behind. ADR% (20-day average of high ÷ low − 1) comes from: —. $ volume averages price × shares over the last 10 or 20 sessions. Group figures are the median (or market-cap-weighted average) of the group's liquid members. Auto-added names and auto groups come from the co-movement test (daily moves with the market's move removed, 120 sessions, 0.6+ score with the group, market cap ≥ $700M and 700K+ shares a day), recomputed weekly, currently with data through —.
