How the grades work (A / B / C / F, inflection, EP, group rank)

Grade = how solid the news behind the gap is, from reading the actual filings, releases and earnings: A real fundamental change (earnings beat with raised guidance, big growth inflection, FDA approval, takeover), B decent (meaningful contract, partnership, index add), C weak (analyst call, sympathy move, hype, no clear news), F warning (offering or other dilution, promotional release, reverse split, unconfirmed news). For gap downs the grade means how real and lasting the bad news is: A serious damage (guidance cut, big miss, lost customer), B meaningful but contained, C weak or no real news (possible overreaction). Inflection = a change in the business trend: Turnaround (a decline reverses), Acceleration (growth speeds up), New business (a new product or customer that changes the company), Margin (profits jump on cost or mix), Deterioration (the trend breaks down); scored 1–10 for how big and how confirmed it is. The top 8 gap ups and top 4 gap downs by pre-market $ volume are graded, and only A and B grades show unless you pick Show all. If fewer stocks pass your rules, only those are researched; if none pass, nothing is. EP = gap of 10%+ with pre-market volume already 25%+ of a normal day (Qullamaggie's episodic pivot asks for a 10%+ gap on huge early volume). Gap ÷ ADR shows how big the gap is for that stock. vs 200D and vs 50 EMA = the pre-market price at scan time vs the 200-day SMA and 50-day EMA as of yesterday's close. Pre-market prices come from Yahoo's 5-minute bars at scan time; check live prints in TradingView. Group rank = the stock's best-ranked group (its themes and its industry) after yesterday's close: ★ Top = top 3% of its kind, Strong = top 3–10%; it isn't part of the news grade (in 2 years it didn't change how many gaps faded on the day, but Top-group gaps ran 20%+ within 20 days 44% of the time vs 28–29% outside the top 10%). Under it: the group and the stock's place in it (its own 1-, 3- and 6-month moves vs the group's other stocks); #1 in group = the leader of a group of 5+ stocks (in a Top group these gaps ran 20%+ 61% of the time vs 41% for the rest of the group, but swung harder both ways).

The rules of this list
How the grade works

The grade is your setup scanner's (MSF engine), set after each close. It grades the trend and the last pullback, not the day's move or volume.

  • First the trend has to line up: 10 EMA above the 21 EMA above the 50 EMA, and the price above the 50 EMA and the 200-day SMA. If not, no grade (—).
  • A+ its last pullback from the 20-day high was 10–15%.
  • A a pullback of up to 20% (other than 10–15%).
  • B a pullback of 20–30%, only for stocks with an ADR of 8%+.

So a stock can be A+ here and B on the Launches tab: that one grades the launch day itself (MIR on Oct 29, 2026: A+ here for its trend and 10.7% pullback, B there for a +11.5% day and a base breakout but only a 1% gap on 1.9× volume).

About these lists

    Launch grade: the launch day itself

    • B two of the four ingredients.
    • C the rest.
    • You your pick (the Follow button on the Watchlist tab): no launch day to grade.

    During the day (Launching today and its alerts) the volume is the pace so far for the time of day, so the grade can change by the close: the Launches table uses the full day (MIR on Oct 29, 2026: A at 11:28 AM on a 3.0× pace, B at the close on 1.9×).

    Breakout grade: a followed launch clearing its buy point (alerts)

    • C the rest (45% / 17%).

    Tags and status on the Launches table

    • catalyst a launch on earnings or news (the earnings / news tags): a 5%+ gap that closed net positive, still in the gap (over the day before's high) and over at least its 1-month base, though up less than 5% or not in the top half of its range (GMAB Aug 7, 2026: guidance raised at 3:52 PM the day before, a 5.4% gap, +4.8%). A launch like any other, followed the same way; it never replaces a regular launch.
    • cleared 6-mo base the biggest base the launch day closed over, by the candle bodies (each day the higher of its open and close): 1, 3 or 6 months.
    • Leg: the launch is leg 1 when it cleared at least its 1-month base, otherwise leg 0 until the first breakout out of its base. A new leg needs a decisive breakout, a close half an ADR or more over the top; a weaker one is a breakout within the same leg (GMAB: Aug 7 leg 1, Aug 14 and Sep 18 breakouts within leg 1, Oct 5 leg 2, Oct 23 leg 3).
    • retest held once per breakout, after a real pullback (1.5 ADRs off the high since the breakout, having closed 1.25 ADRs away first; drifting at the base high is no retest): the low comes back to the broken base's top, by the candle bodies or the wicks (a low that stays over it never touched it), on the day that makes the pullback's low, and the close holds over the level and the 21 EMA: the undercut and reclaim (GMAB Oct 2, 2026: Oct 1 touched and closed just under 34.63; Oct 2 down to 33.72, closed 34.75 over it and the 21 EMA). Closing under the 21 EMA is no retest (ANF Aug 17–18, 2026); days after it without a new low are consolidation. + 21 EMA = the 10, 21 or 50 EMA is at the level too: the confluence (SPOT Oct 28: 536.98–540.82 with the 21 EMA). Alerts: a heads-up when it pulls back into the zone during the day, "retest holding into the close" from 3:45 PM (the buy is at the close), and the confirmation after the close.
    • Push out of the 21 EMA with a gap: within 5 sessions of the retest's touch, while it still sits at the EMAs, it opens over the day before's candle (its open and close) and its 10 & 21 EMA: an entry, alerted at the open (ANF Aug 21, 2026 after touching 105.16 on Aug 17; GMAB Oct 5).

    How launches, legs, buy points, alert grades and tags work

    How a launch is found (after each close, from 2 years of daily bars): the stock opened up, closed up 5%+ in the top half of the day's range on 1.5×+ its 20-day average volume, closed above the 200 SMA, and was still near it the day before (at most 28% above it: a neglected stock or one coming out of a correction, not a trend already running), and closed at least 30% above its 52-week low. A power day (volume 4×+, an 8%+ gap, a 10%+ day, closing over its 1-month high) may start up to 50% above the 200 SMA (SMCI on May 3, 2023 launched at an all-time high 32% over it), and need not close in the top half if it holds half its gap (FSLR on Jul 28, 2022); those show as faded, since the day's low is then a tight stop. Grade = the launch itself, from 4 ingredients: volume 3×+, a 3%+ gap, a base breakout (closed above the previous 20 days' high) and a 10%+ day. A+ = your "go day": volume 3×+, a 6%+ gap and a base breakout, jumping from under the 200 SMA to over it; A volume 3×+ and a base breakout with a 3%+ gap or a 10%+ day; B two of the four; C the rest. earnings = it launched on its earnings report (that day, or the evening before); news = the 8:47 pre-market grading gave it an A or B that morning. Leg = the launch is leg 1 when it closed out of its base or cleared at least its 1-month base (by the candle bodies), otherwise leg 0 (in base) until the first breakout out of it (RPD: launched Sep 14 inside its base, leg 1 = the Oct 22–23 breakouts); each later leg is a breakout out of a base, a close more than 0.5% over its top (CAI Oct 27, 2026: 2.4% over 32.89); a close over the top but within 0.5% is a breakout with caution, still a trigger, and its follow-through starts the leg: within 3 sessions it opens over the day before's close and closes more than 0.5% over the top (ARM Apr 17, 2026: 166.73 over 166.69, then Apr 20: leg 2); a close under the top is no breakout; the smaller ~2-month and the larger ~6-month base both count, and breakouts a few days apart are one leg; a flag breakout inside the base is an entry, not a new leg; after a rest of 40+ sessions the next breakout starts a new run at leg 1. Breakouts got worse leg by leg in the history test, so leg 4+ shows as late. Breakout alerts are graded from what separated the base breakouts after launches (2,743 crosses of the top, Aug 2025 – Nov 2026): A = an early breakout (the 1st or 2nd since the launch, within 50 sessions of it) in a ★ Top or Strong group (the group rank, top 10%) with the volume building the 5 days before (63% ran 10%+ in 20 days, 37% ran 20%+); B = early plus one of those two (52% / 22%); C = the rest (45% / 17%). The group rank replaced the old "theme Leading" here (59% / 31% for its A's), and the breakout day's own volume didn't help. After the close the status says how far it closed over the top, in % and in its ADR. double breakout = the base top and a falling line inside the base broken the same day: squeezing under a line from the base's high with higher lows, the days before still under the top (ARM May 20, 2026, out of the line from its May 6 high and the 239.50 top, with a gap up; not GMAB Oct 23, which climbed the right side of a U to its top the day before); a bigger base's high right above is named, a close under it is not out of it yet (VEEV Oct 9: closed 296.13 under its 296.24 main base high). 3rd try = an ascending triangle: the base's high is the 1st try, a 2nd try within a quarter ADR of it after a real pullback, then a shallower pullback (higher lows): the 3rd try usually breaks out (ARM: Apr 24 and May 6, out May 20); a 2nd try that breaks out right away is a U or flat top, not a triangle. Base top = the highest high of the last ~6 months (at least 5 sessions old, with a pullback of 1.25+ of the stock's own ADRs); it is the buy point, and a close over it is a base breakout (RPD on Oct 23, 2026, over its August high of $14.80 on 2.1× volume). The buy point shown is the nearest trigger above the price: your MSF pattern's pivot, the flag line or the base top. Bull flag (trendline) = a bull flag: the top is at least a 1-month high (a 3- or 6-month high counts too), after a run of 4+ of the stock's own ADRs made by the trend into it (not one gap day; a high that only retests an older top is the 2nd try of a flat top, no pole: ARM May 6, 2026); then the high and 4+ pullback days (a base of 5+ days), 1.25-4 ADRs deep, 3+ lower highs in a descending channel (lower highs and lower lows down to the flag's low, no higher low after it: APLD Apr 24-28, 2026), the drift down a controlled, lower-volume pullback (at or under 1.1× the 20-day average volume: not APPN Sep 2026, 1.17×; not ARM Mar 26-30, 1.75×), riding the 10 or 21 EMA (3+ days closing within an ADR of one), never closing more than half an ADR under the 21 EMA, a controlled pullback with no violent drop (a gap down of 0.7+ ADR closing 1+ ADR down on 1.3×+ volume: ARM Apr 27–28, 2026, an ascending triangle, no flag), with a falling line along its highs; a try that pokes half an ADR+ over the line and closes back under it doesn't redraw it (ARM Apr 10 tried, Apr 13 broke the same line at 149.00). Its buy point is tomorrow's value of that line, the alert fires there during the day; the entry is the close over the line and the 10 & 21 EMA (SMCI Jun 28, 2023; TTAN Oct 30, 2026 after its Oct 23 high). Bull pennant (trendline) = the same after the pole, but converging, a symmetrical triangle: lower highs and higher lows (ARM: highs Mar 25 and Apr 1 stepping down, lows Mar 30, Apr 2 and Apr 7 stepping up), the volume after its first low at or under 1.1× average; its falling upper line is the trigger (ARM Apr 13, 2026). Both are an entry inside the base, with more room and less risk than the base top: the new leg comes with the close over the base top (CAI: the flag Sep 14, 2026, the base breakout Sep 15). A base shorter than 5 days is no flag. The status says when the volume dried up the 2 days before the break (ZS Oct 21, 2026). Status = where the stock is after the close: in the base (under the top, no close above the level its leg broke out of: ANF Aug 27 – Oct 16, 2026 under its Aug 26 gap day's high; or pulled back 1.25+ ADRs from its latest high: ARM Apr 27, 2026, day 2 of the base under 237.68), consolidation, surfing the 10 & 21 EMA on dry volume (its resting time: closing within an ADR of both EMAs, above the 50 EMA, no big candle (body half an ADR or less), while the 5-day volume is 0.85 or less of the 50-day; the surfing is what says it is really resting, not acting up; ANF Sep 17 – Oct 2), tight under the buy point, a breakout, running, a retest that held. Pop out of the consolidation (in base) = your entry inside the base, once per base: a regular consolidation an ADR+ under its top, the volume drying up (as above), surfing the 10 and 21 EMA (closing within an ADR of both) above the 50 EMA; the trigger is the pop out of it, the price expanding after the contraction: after a tight candle (its range 0.8 ADR or less and its body half an ADR or less), the next day opens above that candle's open and close and above the 10 and 21 EMA, both rising, pushing out of the surf; the buy point shown is the price the open has to clear (Qullamaggie: a breakout from a tight flag; ANF: Oct 2's tight candle, the pop Oct 5, 2026, opening $136.97; Oct 1 was no tight candle and Oct 2's open faded). tight ×2 = 2+ tight days just under the buy point (within an ADR, tight bodies of half an ADR or less whatever the wicks; sideways, not a staircase up: ANF Oct 15–16 before its Oct 19 breakout); retest held = once per breakout, after a real pullback, the pullback's low at the broken base's top (by the bodies or the wicks) and the close back over it and the 21 EMA, + the 10 / 21 / 50 EMA when it is there too (GMAB Oct 2, SPOT Oct 28, ANF Oct 23); the close holds over the 10 EMA, or the low tested the 21 EMA and the close reclaimed it (ARM Mar 30, 2026 closed at its low under the 10 EMA: no; Apr 7 down under the 21 EMA and back: the retest + 21 EMA); then the push out of the 21 EMA with a gap is an entry too (ANF Aug 21), but not while still under a flag's falling line (ARM Apr 8). 21 EMA retest = an entry in the base, once per pullback: at least 5 days into the base and 1.25+ ADRs off its high, after a controlled pullback (no advance the 3 days before, the closes held over the 21 EMA) the low goes under the 21 EMA and under the open and the close comes back over it in the upper half of the day (TTAN Oct 29, ARM May 18, NTSK Oct 1, VEEV Sep 21, 2026; not VEEV Sep 15, a gap down after 3 up days). breakout undercut & reclaim = after a breakout, 2+ closes back under its level (the broken base's top) while holding over the 50 EMA, then the close back over it, within 5 sessions (CAI Oct 9, 2026: under 28.01 Oct 6–8, closed 29.70); one close under and back is a retest. 21 EMA undercut & reclaim = the same after 2 closes under the 21 EMA (by less than half an ADR: momentum traders out), a strong signal (SMCI Jun 27, 2023, the day before its flag; not APPN Sep 14, 2026, 1.3 ADRs under: a breakdown).     About the data (update times, sources)

    During market hours (9:30 AM–4:10 PM ET) prices update about every 5 minutes from Yahoo quotes (from about 9:55 their volume is corrected against the consolidated tape: Yahoo's live volume runs several % high during the session), and this page checks for new data every 90 seconds on its own. Live 1D is today's change so far; live 1W, 1M and 3M compare today's price with the close 5, 21 and 63 sessions back; Rel vol is relative volume at time: volume from 9:30 to now ÷ the average of the last 20 sessions' volume from 9:30 to the same minute (from 5-minute bars on the consolidated tape via Alpaca, all US exchanges and off-exchange trades), so 2.00× at 11:50 means twice the usual volume by 11:50; it's rough in the first 5 minutes. After the close it's the full day ÷ the previous 20 sessions' average. Everything else (themes and their scores, ADR%, $ volume, 200-day SMA, averages) is rebuilt after each close, around 4:30 PM ET. Newest day comes from: —; earlier days, volume and company details come from Nasdaq's daily list, which runs one session behind. ADR% (20-day average of high ÷ low − 1) comes from: —. $ volume averages price × shares over the last 10 or 20 sessions. Group figures are the median (or market-cap-weighted average) of the group's liquid members. Auto-added names and auto groups come from the co-movement test (daily moves with the market's move removed, 120 sessions, 0.6+ score with the group, market cap ≥ $700M and 700K+ shares a day), recomputed weekly, currently with data through —. Broad industries come from FinanceDatabase; Detailed uses Nasdaq's own industry labels. Data and code: your GitHub repo (radar-data branch).
