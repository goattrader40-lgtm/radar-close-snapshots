Graded gappers (5%+, top 8 up and 4 down by pre-market $ volume)

Gapping 5%+ up or down before the open and passing your rules (market cap $700M+, 30-day average volume 700K+ shares, 20-day average $ volume $20M+, ADR 3%+). Scanned at — ET on — (not today: today's list posts by 9:10 AM ET on weekdays).

Grade = how solid the news behind the gap is, from reading the actual filings, releases and earnings: A real fundamental change (earnings beat with raised guidance, big growth inflection, FDA approval, takeover), B decent (meaningful contract, partnership, index add), C weak (analyst call, sympathy move, hype, no clear news), F warning (offering or other dilution, promotional release, reverse split, unconfirmed news). For gap downs the grade means how real and lasting the bad news is: A serious damage (guidance cut, big miss, lost customer), B meaningful but contained, C weak or no real news (possible overreaction). Inflection = a change in the business trend: Turnaround (a decline reverses), Acceleration (growth speeds up), New business (a new product or customer that changes the company), Margin (profits jump on cost or mix), Deterioration (the trend breaks down); scored 1–10 for how big and how confirmed it is. The top 8 gap ups and top 4 gap downs by pre-market $ volume are graded, and only A and B grades show unless you pick Show all. If fewer stocks pass your rules, only those are researched; if none pass, nothing is. EP = gap of 10%+ with pre-market volume already 25%+ of a normal day (Qullamaggie's episodic pivot asks for a 10%+ gap on huge early volume). Gap ÷ ADR shows how big the gap is for that stock. Pre-market prices come from Yahoo's 5-minute bars at scan time; check live prints in TradingView.

Launches (20 days)

History test

How a launch is found (after each close, from 2 years of daily bars): the stock opened up, closed up 5%+ in the top half of the day's range on 1.5×+ its 20-day average volume, closed above the 200 SMA, and was still near it the day before (at most 28% above it: a neglected stock or one coming out of a correction, not a trend already running), and closed at least 30% above its 52-week low. A power day (volume 4×+, a 10%+ gap, a 15%+ day) may start up to 50% above the 200 SMA (SMCI on May 3, 2023 launched at an all-time high 32% over it), and need not close in the top half if it holds half its gap (FSLR on Jul 28, 2022); those show as faded, since the day's low is then a tight stop. Grade = the launch itself, from 4 ingredients: volume 3×+, a 3%+ gap, a base breakout (closed above the previous 20 days' high) and a 10%+ day. A+ = your "go day": volume 3×+, a 6%+ gap and a base breakout, jumping from under the 200 SMA to over it; A volume 3×+ and a base breakout with a 3%+ gap or a 10%+ day; B two of the four; C the rest. earnings = it launched on its earnings report (that day, or the evening before); news = the 8:47 pre-market grading gave it an A or B that morning. Leg = the launch is leg 1; each later leg is a decisive breakout out of a base (above its base top) or out of a flag that formed at the highs, one leg per base; after a rest of 40+ sessions the next breakout starts a new run at leg 1. Breakouts got worse leg by leg in the history test, so leg 4+ shows as late. Base top = the highest high of the last ~6 months (at least 5 sessions old, with a pullback of 1.25+ of the stock's own ADRs); its buy point sits a fifth of an ADR above it, and a decisive close over it is a base breakout and a new leg (RPD on Oct 26, 2026, through its August high). The buy point shown is the nearest trigger above the price: your MSF pattern's pivot, the flag line or the base top. Flag (trendline) = after a 25%+ rise, a 5-20 session pullback 1.25-4 of the stock's own ADRs deep that stays above the 50 EMA, with a falling line along its lower highs; its buy point is tomorrow's value of that line (SMCI, June 2023). delayed = a gap of 3%+ on 3×+ volume that faded, held its old base and then launched within 10 sessions (ACN: earnings gap Oct 1, 2026 faded, base held 4 days, launched Oct 8); it is graded with the gap day's volume and gap. follows ACN = a smaller breakout in a leader's wake: within 10 sessions of an A or B launch by another member of its theme, it closed up 2%+ above its 20-day high on 2×+ volume, with the same 200 SMA and 52-week-low rules (DXC on Oct 8, 2026 after ACN). The theme is shown beside it, not in it: a leader often launches before its theme (DELL on Feb 27, 2026 while semis were still correcting; its group led by April), so a launch counts as a lone wolf only when none of its groups has trended since. Exit signal = the first close below the 21 EMA. Patterns, buy points, base days and depth come from your MSF engine (the same as the 3-SETUPS list); Surf = closes in a row above the 10 / 21 EMA. During market hours, Launching today checks the watch list (stocks from 10% under to 28% over their 200 SMA, and up to 50% over it for power days) against the live 5-minute prices: up 5%+, opened up, above the 200 SMA, Rel vol 1.5×+ for this time of day. Entry is yours to time (opening-range high), stop under the day's low.

During market hours (9:30 AM–4:10 PM ET) prices update about every 5 minutes from Yahoo quotes, and this page checks for new data every 2 minutes on its own. Live 1D is today's change so far; live 1W, 1M and 3M compare today's price with the close 5, 21 and 63 sessions back; Rel vol is relative volume at time: volume from 9:30 to now ÷ the average of the last 20 sessions' volume from 9:30 to the same minute (from 5-minute bars on the consolidated tape via Alpaca, all US exchanges and off-exchange trades), so 2.00× at 11:50 means twice the usual volume by 11:50; it's rough in the first 5 minutes. After the close it's the full day ÷ the previous 20 sessions' average. Everything else (themes and their scores, ADR%, $ volume, 200-day SMA, averages) is rebuilt after each close, around 4:30 PM ET.
